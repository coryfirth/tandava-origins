# Everything is crawlable on purpose. The unlisted pages (/tools/,
# /intake/lifestyle/, /thanks/lifestyle/, /koya/, /privacy/) keep
# themselves out of search with <meta name="robots" content="noindex">,

# here would block the fetch, the tag would never be read, and the URLs
# could still surface in results with no description under them. So they
# stay crawlable and keep saying noindex for themselves.
#
