Questions people ask us

Is there any other cost on top of the price?+

There are no additional costs on top of the kits. If you want to go deeper with coaching or additional programming you can choose to select those after you receive your results.

What will this tell me that I don’t already know?+

Most of our clients already know a lot about their health. What they’re missing is the translation: how their genetics and their habits fit together, and what to do with it. Your report looks long, but by the end it’s very targeted.

Is this a diagnosis?+

No. Everything in our kits is a tendency. It’s not about disease, it doesn’t diagnose anything, and it doesn’t replace your doctor.

Does my DNA change? Should I wait?+

Your DNA doesn’t change. This is your baseline, the genetics you were born with. What you do in your real life shapes how it shows up, which is why we read it next to how you live now.

Who sees my data?+

You do, and on Practitioner-Guided, your practitioner. Your sample is processed by our accredited lab partner (HIPAA validated, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR audited). Your raw file is yours to download, and you can ask us to delete your data.

Where do you ship?+

Our kits are available in the United States, Canada, Mexico and Europe. Your kit ships to your door with a prepaid return box. We can ship to other countries by request.

I’m not great with technology.+

That’s fine. We can walk you through the portal on a free technical call to make sure you have access to your results easily.

Will the changes actually stick?+

We focus on high-impact, low-effort changes, a minimum routine you can keep, introduced gradually so it never feels all or nothing. It works to the extent you take it seriously.

What if it doesn’t work for me?+

We don’t promise outcomes. Your results describe tendencies, and the early signs are usually small: a fact that finally makes sense, one habit that’s easier than you expected, a night that feels a little better. Slow, foundational incremental changes are more impactful over time that big, harder to achieve ones. We focus there so you see the results immediately.
