What Is Lifestyle Design?

Lifestyle design is the art and science of building your daily life around your own biology, so you feel, think and perform in line with your health goals.

The better you understand how your body works, the easier it is to build habits that actually stick, and your genes are one of the clearest places to start.

How Your Genetics Inform Your Design.

Lifestyle genomics is the study of how your genes shape the way your body responds to how you live: what you eat, how you sleep, how you move and how you handle stress. It’s why two people can follow the same plan and get completely different results.

Your results show where you land in each of these five areas, so you know which changes are worth making first.
